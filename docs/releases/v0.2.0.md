## The Order: 1886 — v0.2.0

![The Order: 1886 running on PC](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JulioCacko/The-Order-1886_pc/v0.2.0/docs/images/preview9/hero.jpg)

The first full release of the Go launcher. The geometry that exploded while aiming is fixed, the smoke grenade draws its cloud, black flecks are gone from faces, the game no longer freezes mid-fight on a runaway draw, and new shaders compile in the background instead of stalling the game. The launcher adds AMD FSR 3.1 and NVIDIA DLAA in place of the game's own anti-aliasing, and shows trophy names from your own game.

This release ships the Go launcher only; the old Tkinter launcher and its files are gone. It is still compatibility software: full progression, long sessions and 4K/60 are not accepted yet, and **short black flashes still appear during gameplay** (see the end of these notes).

### Fixed — before and after

**Aiming no longer explodes the scene (#106).** Aiming used to throw stretched, garbage geometry across the screen, which is why v0.2.0 was held. The aim-focus readback now publishes its result without stopping the GPU mid-frame, so CPU-written vertices are no longer lost.

![Before: stretched geometry while aiming. After: the Council chamber renders correctly.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JulioCacko/The-Order-1886_pc/v0.2.0/docs/images/fixes/aim-watch-explosion-before-after.jpg)

**The smoke grenade draws its cloud (#107).** The MK IV-S smoke grenade went off with no smoke. Its tessellated particles were lost to three shader-translation gaps (passthrough hull shaders, the instance base, and 64-bit-address buffer loads).

![Before: the grenade goes off with no cloud. After: the smoke cloud fills the room.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JulioCacko/The-Order-1886_pc/v0.2.0/docs/images/fixes/smoke-grenade-cloud-before-after.jpg)

**No more black flecks on faces (#101).** Close-ups in chapter II showed black specks on skin: the lighting pass never shaded those pixels. Re-drawn surfaces now get a small depth bias.

![Before: black flecks on a face in close-up. After: clean skin shading.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JulioCacko/The-Order-1886_pc/v0.2.0/docs/images/fixes/face-flecks-before-after.jpg)

**Other fixes**
- The game no longer stops presenting mid-fight (#103). An indirect draw asked the GPU for about 4.3 billion indices; index counts are now clamped to the bound index buffer.
- Aiming keeps the target sharp (#100): the autofocus buffers are read back fresh, so depth of field focuses where you aim.

### New

- **AMD FSR 3.1 and NVIDIA DLAA** (#97, #98) replace the game's temporal anti-aliasing at native resolution, then FSR 1 scales to your output. Choose them on Graphics → Upscaling. DLAA needs a GeForce RTX GPU.
- **Shaders compile off the GPU thread by default** (#105). New effects no longer stall the frame; a draw whose shader is still compiling is skipped for that frame. Turn off Performance → Asynchronous shaders to wait instead.
- **Trophy names** (#99). Enter your own console trophy key on Achievements and the launcher decrypts trophy names and descriptions from your game.
- **Go launcher only** (#108). The retired Tkinter launcher's files, the leftovers inherited from the God of War project, and its stale documents were removed. The bundled QUICKSTART.txt now describes the current launcher.

### Known issue: black flashes

During gameplay the screen sometimes flashes black for a moment. It was seen in every test session with the launcher defaults. Whether another setting avoids it was not established, and the cause has not been found; a geometry (vertex) problem is suspected.

### What this release does not claim

- **Not accepted yet:** full progression, long-session stability, Chapter 6/9 QTE timing above 30 FPS, and sustained 4K/60.
- **Smoke and aiming together:** each fix was verified on its own; a played session that throws smoke while aiming with both fixes was not recorded.
- **Linux:** the package starts; gameplay on a Linux GPU driver is untested.
- **Upscalers:** FSR 3.1 and DLAA run at native size. DLSS quality modes, XeSS and FSR 4 are not offered yet.
