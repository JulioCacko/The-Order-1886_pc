## The Order: 1886 — v0.2.1 (launcher hotfix)

A launcher-only hotfix on top of [v0.2.0](https://github.com/JulioCacko/The-Order-1886_pc/releases/tag/v0.2.0). The runtime is unchanged.

### Saves you can find and bring in

Game & Content → **Saves** now shows where your saves are kept and has two new buttons:

- **Open saves folder** opens `user\order1886\savedata` (it is created if the game has not saved yet).
- **Import saves…** lets you choose a folder and copies its saves in. It accepts another installation's `savedata` folder, its `CUSA00035` folder, a save backup, or a single save folder (`Playthrough00` beside `Playthrough00.sce_sys`). Your current saves are backed up first, and only saves with the same name are replaced. Then start the game and choose Continue or Load.

Saves copied straight off a PS4 are encrypted (a save image next to a `.bin` key file). The launcher now says so instead of failing silently: decrypt them first, for example with Apollo Save Tool on a PS4.

### Only options that work are shown

Settings that are not available yet are hidden instead of shown greyed out: FSR 4 and XeSS, upscaler quality presets, frame generation, vibration, input remapping, separate text and voice language, and the cheat toggles. They will appear when they work.

### Unchanged from v0.2.0

The runtime and its known issue: short black flashes still appear during gameplay, and their cause has not been found yet.
