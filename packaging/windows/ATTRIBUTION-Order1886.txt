The Order: 1886 PC compatibility project

This project reuses infrastructure from FelipePrado0/gow3_pc
(https://github.com/FelipePrado0/gow3_pc), licensed under GPL-2.0-or-later.
It includes components derived from shadPS4
(https://github.com/shadps4-emu/shadPS4). Preserve the accompanying LICENSE
and the applicable source and dependency notices when redistributing.

These credits identify the origin of reused software. They do not establish
compatibility, endorsement, or ownership of The Order: 1886 game content.
The portable package excludes extracted game artwork and game archives.

The optional experimental timing recipe derives from illusion's published
The Order 1886 patch in PS-Game-Patch, commit
3ad781ad8748139d2ca5e6c3b018d0063b8aa1a3:
https://github.com/illusionyy/PS-Game-Patch/blob/3ad781ad8748139d2ca5e6c3b018d0063b8aa1a3/patches/xml/TheOrder1886-Orbis.xml
That source declares app version 01.02 and warns of QTE softlocks in Chapters
6 and 9. This project's opt-in recipe separately verifies the exact current
executable hashes and instruction before changing a derived prepared image.
It does not modify original game files or establish stable 60 FPS gameplay.
